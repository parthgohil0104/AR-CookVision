# AR Marker Placement Guide

## Overview

The AR marker is a printed image used as a **tracking reference** for the AR-CookVision app. It tells the app where to anchor virtual cooking overlays in your real kitchen. The marker itself contains no cooking instructions — it is purely a visual tracking target.

---

## Printing the Marker

### File

Use `public/targets/marker-print.png` for printing.

### Recommended Print Size

- **8–10 cm × 8–10 cm** (approximately 3–4 inches)
- Print on plain white paper or card stock
- Use a laser printer for best contrast (inkjet is acceptable)
- Do **not** scale or distort the image — print at 1:1 square aspect ratio

### Paper

- Use matte paper or card stock to reduce glare
- Glossy paper may cause reflections that interfere with tracking
- Laminating is acceptable if matte lamination is used

---

## Placement

### Recommended Surface

Place the marker on a **flat, dry, clean surface** beside your stove or cooking area.

Good surfaces:
- Kitchen countertop
- Cutting board (placed to the side, not in use)
- A small stand or clip holder

### Position

- Place the marker **beside** the stove, **not under** or **on top of** the pan
- The marker should be clearly visible to the smartphone camera during cooking
- Angle the marker so it faces the approximate direction you will hold your phone

### Minimum Safe Distance from Heat

- Keep the marker **at least 30 cm (12 inches)** from any open flame or heat source
- Keep it away from the edge of burners and hot surfaces
- If using a gas stove, ensure the marker is well clear of the flame ring

---

## Safety Guidelines

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT: Fire and burn hazards exist in every kitchen. Follow these rules strictly.**

### Marker Safety

- ❌ **Never** place the marker under, on, or near a hot pan
- ❌ **Never** place the marker where oil or grease could splash onto it
- ❌ **Never** place the marker on the stove surface or burner grate
- ❌ **Never** place the marker where steam will directly condense on it
- ✅ Place the marker on a dry counter surface beside the cooking area
- ✅ Keep the marker flat and unobstructed

### Phone Safety

- ❌ **Never** hold the phone directly above a flame or hot pan
- ❌ **Never** place the phone on the stove or burner
- ❌ **Never** rest the phone on a surface where it could slide into a hot zone
- ✅ Hold the phone at a comfortable distance, pointing toward the marker
- ✅ Use the phone from a safe standing position beside the stove
- ✅ If using a phone mount, position it well away from heat

### Visibility

- Ensure the marker is well-lit (kitchen lighting is usually sufficient)
- Avoid placing the marker in direct harsh sunlight which may wash out the pattern
- Avoid excessive glare from overhead lights reflecting off the marker surface
- Keep the marker clear of steam — if steam from cooking obscures the marker, reposition it further from the stove or use an exhaust fan

### Hygiene

- Replace the printed marker if it becomes stained with food, oil, or water
- Do not touch the marker with greasy or wet hands
- Keep a few spare printed copies

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Marker not detected | Ensure the full marker is visible and well-lit |
| Tracking is jittery | Move the marker to a more stable, flat surface |
| Marker gets wet/oily | Reprint and replace the marker |
| Steam covers the marker | Reposition further from the stove or use exhaust fan |
| Glare on marker | Switch to matte paper or adjust lighting angle |

---

## Summary

1. Print `marker-print.png` at 8–10 cm square on matte paper
2. Place on a flat, dry surface beside (not on) the stove
3. Keep at least 30 cm from any flame or heat source
4. Keep visible to the phone camera
5. Replace if damaged, wet, or stained
